# LAFU Gift Cards for WooCommerce

Gift cards for WooCommerce. Sell them online and at the counter, take them at checkout and see every cent in a full history.

- Three ready designs (Colour in your brand colour, Gift ribbon, Christmas), picked on the product with a live preview
- Customers pick the amount, the recipient, a message and the delivery day
- Cards pay like a payment method, fully or in part, in the block and classic checkout
- Sell cards at the counter as normal orders, import old paper cards from CSV
- A gift card page and email for the recipient, with QR code and print or save as PDF
- Full history of every card, refunds back to the card
- 20 languages, WPML and Polylang, HPOS and block checkout ready

**Try it:** [live demo](https://www.lanetti.fi/demo/giftcards) · **Guide:** [picture guide and course](https://www.lanetti.fi/tutorials/gift-cards-getting-started) · **Product page:** [lanetti.fi/products/gift-cards](https://www.lanetti.fi/products/gift-cards)

LAFU Gift Cards Pro adds the Designer (your own words, colours and pictures on every card), the till with QR scanning, printed cards, store credit, top-ups, bulk orders, group gifts, photo designs, reports and more.

## Install

1. Download the latest release zip from [Releases](../../releases) or use the code here as the `lafu-gift-cards` folder.
2. In WordPress, Plugins, Add New, Upload Plugin, choose the zip and activate it. WooCommerce must be active.
3. Add a product, tick Gift card, pick a design on its Gift card tab and follow the [guide](https://www.lanetti.fi/tutorials/gift-cards-getting-started).

Requires WordPress 6.5+, WooCommerce and PHP 7.4+.
